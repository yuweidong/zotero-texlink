TexLink Structured formula anchors for Zotero TexLink turns native PDF image annotations into traceable formula blocks in Zotero notes. Recognition runs locally in a Web Worker and never uses an external OCR service or LLM. Changes are saved immediately. The master switch and startup warmup take effect after the next Zotero restart.
